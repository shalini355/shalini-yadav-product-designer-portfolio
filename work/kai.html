Kai · Wellness

An empathetic AI wellness companion.

I designed and developed Kai on my own. It offers English and Hinglish chat, mood-trend charts, and a friendly fallback when the AI is unavailable.

Overview

Role
Designer and developer · Solo project

The problem

Wellness support should feel easy to approach.

Kai is designed to offer an empathetic wellness companion, with chat that can feel familiar in either English or Hinglish.

Who it's for

People looking for a little wellness support.

Kai is for people who want to chat in English or Hinglish and keep their personal data private.

What I made

A companion for chat and mood reflection.

  • English and Hinglish chat
  • Sentiment scoring
  • Mood-trend charts
  • Friendly AI-unavailable fallback
  • Secure sign-in
  • Privacy-first data handling

Design decisions

Small choices that make the experience feel more considered.

English and Hinglish chat

I included both languages so people can use the language that feels natural to them.

Mood trends

I used sentiment scoring and mood-trend charts to make changes in mood visible over time.

A friendly fallback

If the AI is unavailable, a friendly fallback keeps the experience from ending with a dead end.

Privacy-first sign-in

I included secure sign-in and privacy-first handling because personal wellness data needs care.

What I learned

An experience should feel easy and kind to use.

Building Kai showed me that what I love most is making a product feel easy and kind to use.
