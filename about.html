About

Art, code, and finding my way to design.

My journey into design began long before I wrote my first line of code. Immersed in fine arts and fashion design from an early age, I developed a natural sensitivity to texture, balance, colour psychology, and visual hierarchy.

When I entered my Computer Science degree at PSIT Kanpur, I realised that modern software often lacks genuine human warmth. I decided to channel my technical foundation and artistic background into product design — crafting digital interfaces that don't just function efficiently, but make people feel understood and cared for.
