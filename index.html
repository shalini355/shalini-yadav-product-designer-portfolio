Aspiring Product Designer & CS Student

Where computer science meets color, craft, and human empathy.

My journey into design began long before writing my first line of code. Immersed in fine arts and fashion design from an early age, I developed a natural sensitivity to texture, balance, color psychology, and visual hierarchy.
