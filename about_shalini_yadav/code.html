Core Sensibility

Philosophy • Haute Logic

Where the tactile precision of fashion meets the scalable logic of software.

My career began not in lines of code or vector nodes, but over broad drafting tables dusted with tailor’s chalk in New Delhi. Cutting patterns for bespoke haute couture, I developed a visceral, hands-on fascination with how cloth drapes across moving anatomy, how tension creates silhouette, and how people emotionally inhabit physical artifacts.

When I transitioned into digital interface design and distributed product architecture—first across Bangalore’s high-velocity tech ecosystem, and later collaborating across San Francisco—I found that digital systems suffer from the same vulnerability as ill-fitted garments: an excess of cold geometric rigidity, detached from human temperament.

Today, I lead staff-level product design initiatives bridging 0-to-1 zero-gravity ideation with enterprise-scale design tokens. Whether architecting high-frequency financial interfaces or sovereign wellness platforms, I construct experiences that honor human intuition while scaling deterministically under the hood.
